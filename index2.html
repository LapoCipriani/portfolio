About Me

Background

My relationship with computers started very early. I was two years old when my father let me play Centurio, sparking a natural curiosity for the digital world. In second grade, I created my first hypertext: a collection of pages about the animals in my school’s garden, with one page for each animal.
As I grew up, I learned to use Excel, Word, and Paint, exploring the web and experimenting with creative tools. During my teenage years, the computer became mainly a space for music: I worked with Guitar Pro, Audacity, and Cubasis, and with Foobar I wrote small scripts to automatically sort my music collection and customize the interface to my needs.
Later, I installed Ubuntu Studio in dual boot and started using JACK for audio management. At the same time, I helped friends and acquaintances solve small computer problems.
At university, I considered enrolling in Computer Science but chose DAMS instead, where I graduated with a thesis on usability and SEO for rehearsal studio websites, combining my passion for art with my interest in digital technologies. During my studies, I also took an HTML exam, further consolidating a passion that has never left me.
Today, I’m following the Full-Stack Web Developer course at Start2Impact and have started building my first web projects, creating a portfolio that showcases my journey and the skills I’ve developed.
